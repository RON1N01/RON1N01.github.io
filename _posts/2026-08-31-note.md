# DEF CON 34: Volunteering, Learning, and Exploring

This year, I attended **DEF CON 34 in Las Vegas**, and one of the best parts of the experience was getting to participate rather than simply attend.

## Volunteering

I volunteered with both **Red Team Village and Blue Team Village**.

At Red Team Village, I helped with setup and event operations, including attendee traffic flow. At Blue Team Village, I greeted attendees, answered questions, provided directions and information, assisted with badge sales, and helped people as they entered and exited the village.

Volunteering gave me a different perspective on DEF CON. I wasn't only there to learn. I got to contribute to the community and meet some great people along the way.

## Exploring DEF CON

Outside of volunteering, I explored the villages and learned about areas of security beyond what I normally work with and study.

**Quantum Village** especially stood out to me. I've developed an interest in physics and quantum mechanics, so seeing the intersection between quantum computing, cryptography, and cybersecurity was fascinating. I didn't understand everything, and that was part of what made it exciting. It showed me another area with a lot left to explore.

## The Experience

The people were one of my favorite parts of DEF CON. I met volunteers, security professionals, researchers, and others who were simply curious and excited to share what they knew.

DEF CON 34 reinforced something important for me: learning doesn't only happen through books, certifications, or labs. Sometimes it happens by volunteering, meeting someone new, asking questions, or walking into a village where you know almost nothing.

I left DEF CON with new ideas, new people to learn from, and an even longer list of things I want to explore.
